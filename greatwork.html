GreatWork Global

Website Preview

About the Project

GreatWork Global is a modern workspace solutions partner designed to help local startups and global enterprises establish a physical presence in the Philippines. The platform showcases fully equipped private suites, co-working desks, and virtual office addresses in premium commercial locations.

The goal was to build a premium, conversion-focused website that communicates the quality and professionalism of the physical spaces — making international clients feel confident about renting space they've never visited in person.

The Challenge

GreatWork's target audience is split between local startups and international businesses — two groups with very different expectations and browsing behaviors. The website needed to communicate luxury and trust to enterprise clients while also being approachable for smaller teams.

Co-developing the site alongside another designer required careful version control and handoff within Wix Studio, ensuring design consistency and avoiding conflicts during the build phase.

Key Features

Location Pages

Dedicated pages for each premium office location with photos, amenities, and availability info.

Space Showcase

Visual galleries presenting co-working desks, private suites, and meeting rooms in an elegant layout.

Inquiry System

Smart contact forms with service-type selection to route inquiries to the right team efficiently.

Virtual Office Plans

Clear pricing tiers for virtual office addresses, making it easy for remote teams to get a local PH address.

Tech Stack

Built in Wix Studio with a focus on high-quality imagery, smooth animations, and cross-browser compatibility — co-developed with a teammate while maintaining consistent design standards throughout.
