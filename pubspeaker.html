PubSpeaker

An AI-driven mobile app that helps users become better public speakers through real-time feedback and progress tracking.

Get The App
App Preview

About the Project

PubSpeaker is a speech analysis and feedback application designed to help users improve their public speaking and pronunciation skills. The app transcribes spoken input into text, analyzes pronunciation, grammar, and fluency, and highlights errors or areas for improvement.

Users can review past speech results through a history feature, allowing them to reread transcripts or replay their recorded speech for self-evaluation. The goal is to give anyone — from students to professionals — a private, judgment-free tool to practice and grow their communication confidence.

The Challenge

Public speaking anxiety affects a huge number of people, yet most practice tools are either too expensive, require a human coach, or lack real-time actionable feedback. The challenge was to build a lightweight mobile experience that feels encouraging and personal — not clinical or intimidating.

We also needed to handle the complexity of real-time speech transcription and AI-generated feedback within a React Native environment, ensuring the app remained fast and responsive on Android.

Key Features

Real-Time Transcription

Converts speech to text instantly as the user speaks, with minimal latency.

AI-Driven Feedback

Analyzes grammar, fluency, and pronunciation, providing personalized suggestions for improvement.

Session History

Users can revisit past sessions, review transcripts, and track their progress over time.

User Profiles

Personalized profiles to store preferences, session data, and improvement milestones.

Tech Stack

Built with a cross-platform approach to ensure consistent performance on Android, with a Python backend handling the speech processing and AI model integration.
